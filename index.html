Privacy policy

In the West Hills on Android · Boundless Studios · Updated November 22, 2026

Playing the game

Your character name, shop name, progress, and settings are saved in the game’s storage on your device. The game runs without a Boundless Studios account. The game does not send your saves or gameplay activity to Boundless Studios, display ads, or include third-party analytics. We do not sell or share player information from the game.

Your purchase

Google Play processes your purchase and manages purchase history under Google’s privacy policy. The game does not request or store your payment card details.

Managing your data

Your local game files remain until you remove them. To erase the game’s local data and settings, open Android Settings, choose Apps, select In the West Hills, then choose Storage and clear the app’s data. This deletes your local progress. The Android app disables automatic backup of its files.

Contact

If you email us for support or to request deletion of a previous message, we receive the address and information you send. We use this information to handle your request. You can reach Boundless Studios at admin@magicstudios.ai.
